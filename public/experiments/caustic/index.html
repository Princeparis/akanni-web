Under the surface. The idea, the maths & the source ↙
Simple rules. Unexpected light.

A surface remembers
where you have been.

Two floating-point textures exchange height and velocity. Each point feels its four neighbours; your pointer adds an impulse. The resulting height gradient bends the poster underneath it. Red, green, and blue sample slightly different positions to imitate dispersion. Procedural highlights suggest concentrated light; this is an artistic optical model, not a ray-traced fluid solver.

Download the complete working source ↓ 
laplacian = left + right + above + below - 4 * height;
velocity = (velocity + laplacian * 0.24) * damping;
height += velocity;

normal = gradient(height);
refractedUV = uv + normal * refraction;
colour = sampleRGB(poster, refractedUV, dispersion);
